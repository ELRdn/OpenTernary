# OpenTernary name and logo

The source code of OpenTernary is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
That license does **not** grant rights to the project's name or logo (Apache-2.0,
Section 6). This file explains how they may be used.

## What counts as the marks

- The name **OpenTernary** and the `OPEN-TERNARY` wordmark.
- The OpenTernary symbol (the three-tomoe mark) in any form, including the files in
  `docs/brand/` and the terminal rendering baked into `src/openternary/ui/assets/`.

The files in `docs/brand/` and `src/openternary/ui/assets/` are **not** covered by the
Apache License. All rights are reserved, except as allowed below.

## Allowed without asking

- Redistributing **unmodified** OpenTernary releases, which contain the marks.
- Referring to the project by name, e.g. "based on OpenTernary", "a fork of OpenTernary",
  "works with OpenTernary".
- Showing the logo next to such a reference, unmodified, without suggesting endorsement.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Naming a fork, product or service "OpenTernary" or a confusingly similar name.
- Using the logo or wordmark as the identity of a fork, product or service.
- Suggesting that a modified version is official or endorsed by the OpenTernary project.

A fork that is distributed publicly should replace the name and logo with its own
(change `src/openternary/ui/brand.py` and the assets), while keeping the `NOTICE` file
as required by the Apache License.
